ORTHOPRAXY

Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Orthopraxy is a free app for daily Bible reading and prayer, made by Kyle (Mickey) Micklos. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The short version: what you write in the app stays on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your notes and your answers to the daily question
  • Your prayer list (the names and needs you add under "Pray for")
  • Passages you save and the folders you put them in
  • Your reading plan progress and which days you kept a session
  • Your settings and reminder times

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. Reminders are scheduled by your iPhone itself. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it. Your iPhone's own backups may include it, the same as for any app.

What the app asks the internet for

To show Scripture and the church calendar, the app requests them from two services. These requests contain what is being looked up, not anything about you:

  • Crossway's ESV API (api.esv.org), for English Standard Version text, audio recordings and searches. When you search the ESV, your search words are sent to Crossway to find matching passages. Crossway's own privacy policy is on crossway.org.
  • Orthocal (orthocal.info), for the Orthodox church calendar: the day's readings, commemorations and fasting. Only the date is sent.

Like any website, these services can see the internet address your request comes from. The app sends no name, email, device ID or location.

The King James and Septuagint texts and the writings of the Church Fathers are built into the app, so reading them sends nothing.

Feedback

"Send Feedback" opens a new email in your own mail app, addressed to me. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. The email includes the app's version and your iPhone model so I can find problems; you can delete those lines before sending.

Crash reports

If you have chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports. That is Apple's setting, not the app's, and you can turn it off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever starts using another service (for example, for audio recordings), this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.
