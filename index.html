ORTHOPRAXY

Support

Orthopraxy is a free iPhone app for daily Bible reading and prayer: a short session each day to arrive, read, notice and pray, following a reading plan in the ESV or the Orthodox church calendar.

Get in touch

Questions, a problem, or an idea? In the app, tap the menu (the three lines at the top right) and choose Send Feedback. Or email mickeymicklosfilm@gmail.com.

Orthopraxy is made by one person, so replies may take a few days.

Common questions

  • Where are my notes and prayer list? Only on your phone. Open the menu › Your Notes. The prayer list appears in the Pray step of each session.
  • Can I listen instead of read? ESV readings have a Listen button that plays Crossway's own recording. Other translations don't have audio yet.
  • How do I see what the Church Fathers said? Tap "The Fathers on this chapter" above any chapter, tap Fathers during a session, or select verses and tap "The Fathers on these verses."
  • How do I select several verses? Tap a verse to select it, or press and hold, then drag. Tap Clear, or the passage title, to clear the selection.
  • Light mode, dark mode, purple? Menu › Appearance, and Dark mode color.
